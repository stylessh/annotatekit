The thread panel reads as a compact feedback thread instead of a chat: the note first, then replies and status changes on one timeline with small avatars and no bubbles. Status shows as an icon (neutral by default), status actions are icon buttons in the header, and the reply field is a single pill with attach and send icons. The panel morphs its height when switching views or when replies arrive, slides between the list and a thread, fades scrolled edges instead of cutting them, and new replies settle in.
